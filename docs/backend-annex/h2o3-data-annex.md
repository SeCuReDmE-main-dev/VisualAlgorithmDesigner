# H2O-3 Data Annex Boundary

Visual Algorithm Designer is the product. RaySight is the student-facing guide and mascot. H2O is present as the official `Powered by H2O` visual attribution and as a backend/data annex source, not as the student-facing product identity.

Allowed H2O usage:

- brand attribution text or image: `Powered by H2O`;
- backend adapters, data imports, migrations, or scripts that explicitly manage H2O-3 reference data;
- technical research notes that evaluate H2O-3 as an optional data or execution annex.

Not allowed in the student-facing surface:

- palette titles such as `H2O building blocks`;
- canvas copy such as `H2O visual pipeline`;
- product naming that makes RaySight or H2O the app name;
- provider, endpoint, key, or secret wording that implies H2O-3 is a classroom AI route.

The teaching surface should describe algorithms in adolescent-readable language: purpose, example, history, inputs, outputs, difficulty, and why the algorithm matters.
